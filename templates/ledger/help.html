A little help on the road.

Everything you need to keep your garage organized.

How do I add a car?

Open Cars in the bottom navigation, tap Add Car, and save the details. You can upload your own photo.

Add a car →
How do fuel entries work?

Tap the red + button, choose Fuel, then enter quantity and price per litre. Calculate Total multiplies these fields. Receipt and pump photos are stored with the entry; read the photos and enter the details yourself.

Where are my reminders?

Tap the bell to open your calendar and service reminders. Choose a marked date to see its reminders, or switch to List. Mark an item complete when the service is done.

View reminders →
Can I export my data?

Export Expense Data downloads your complete expense history as CSV. Keep this file and your original photos in your own backups.

Download CSV →
How do I change my login?

Use Account Settings to change your phone number, name, and email. Use Change Password to update your password. If you cannot sign in, ask your app administrator to reset it.

Account settings →
Does this app work offline?

The app can be installed on your home screen. Signing in, viewing records, saving expenses, and uploading photos require an internet connection. Reminders appear in the app; automatic push alerts are not enabled.
